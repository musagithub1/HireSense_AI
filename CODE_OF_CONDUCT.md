# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

HireSense AI welcomes contributors of different backgrounds, identities,
experience levels, and viewpoints. Treat people with respect, assume good
intent, and discuss technical decisions without attacking the person making
them.

Expected behavior includes:

- Giving specific, constructive feedback
- Respecting privacy and consent
- Making space for less experienced contributors
- Correcting mistakes openly
- Keeping hiring, accessibility, and bias concerns grounded in evidence

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized conduct
- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sharing credentials, resumes, recordings, or candidate data
- Using project spaces to promote discriminatory hiring practices

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests,
or other contributions that violate this code. Repeated or serious violations
may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

To report conduct privately, use the repository's GitHub security reporting
flow. If that option is unavailable, open a minimal issue asking a maintainer
for a private contact method. Do not include sensitive details in a public
issue.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and fairly as the available project
tools allow.
